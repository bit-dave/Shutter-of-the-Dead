# Shutter of the Dead

An augmented reality zombie shooter for your phone. Your camera shows your real room, zombies claw up through the floor and smash through your doors and windows, and you aim with the phone and tap to shoot.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link in Safari or Chrome on your phone, tap START, and allow Camera and Motion.
